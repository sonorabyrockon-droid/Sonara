SONORA 3.11 — SHOW MORE CONTROLS

Frontend-only change:
- Music catalogue now renders the first 10 matching records and adds a Show more tracks control in batches of 10.
- Admin activity now renders the first 8 records and adds a Show more activity control in batches of 8.
- Both lists provide Show less after expansion.
- Catalogue search resets its visible batch to the first 10 matches.
- Controls use the existing delegated frontend click handler.
- Added matching desktop and mobile styling.

No Worker, SQL, database schema, API, or deployment files were changed.
